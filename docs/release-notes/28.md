### The longer version

**Features**

- **Animations can be turned off.** A new switch on the home screen and in About stops every drawn animation in the app — the pets, the paw prints, the screen transitions and the spinner on the pairing screen. It works alongside Android's own "remove animations" setting, without needing a trip through Developer options to find it.

**UI/UX**

- **The pets move like animals.** Tails wag in bursts and settle rather than ticking along at one unchanging rate, ears and heads follow the body instead of moving in lockstep with it, and the dog pants in bouts rather than holding its tongue out permanently. Every so often one of them breaks the idle with an ear flick, a head tilt or a sniff.
- **They look at each other.** Each pet watches whichever neighbour it is nearest, glances about, and meets your eye every 15 seconds or so — instead of all three staring straight out of the screen indefinitely.
- **Turning animations off leaves a proper picture.** The scene settles into a composed pose — everyone sitting square, eyes open, the ball resting on the floor — rather than freezing whatever half-finished movement it happened to be in the middle of.

**Bug fixes**

- **The dog can hit its ball.** On the sessions screen the dog swiped at the ball on its own rhythm, unrelated to where the ball actually was, so it looked like it kept missing. Its paw and the ball's bounce now meet.
