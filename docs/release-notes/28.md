### Features

- **Animations can be turned off.** A new switch on the home screen and in About stops every drawn animation in the app — the pets, the paw prints, the screen transitions and the spinner on the pairing screen. Worth having on an older or slower phone, and on a Monitor you leave running all day. It works alongside Android's own "remove animations" setting, but doesn't need you to go digging through Developer options to find it.

### UI/UX

- **The pets move like animals now.** Tails wag in bursts and settle rather than ticking along at one unchanging rate, ears and heads follow the body instead of moving in lockstep with it, and the dog pants in bouts rather than holding its tongue out permanently. They also break the idle every so often with an ear flick, a head tilt or a sniff.
- **They look at each other.** Each pet watches whichever of its neighbours it is nearest, glances around, and meets your eye every so often — instead of all of them staring straight out of the screen the whole time.
- **Turning animations off leaves a proper picture.** The scene settles into a composed pose — everyone sitting square, eyes open, the ball resting on the floor — rather than freezing whatever half-finished movement it happened to be in the middle of.

### Bug Fixes

- **The dog can hit its ball.** On the sessions screen the dog swiped at the ball on its own rhythm, unrelated to where the ball actually was, so it looked like it kept missing. Its paw and the ball's bounce now meet.
