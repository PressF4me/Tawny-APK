### The longer version

**Bug fixes**

- **The camera follows the phone round.** Rotating a Monitor to landscape left it sending an upright, portrait-shaped picture — letterboxed on the viewing phone and wrong on its own preview. It now matches however the phone is held, in both directions. The cause was a self-check that judged the shape of a frame by asking the camera what it had been *asked* for rather than by measuring the picture it actually delivered, so it agreed with itself every time and never corrected. It now measures the delivered frame.
- **Rotating no longer puts the camera light out.** Reshaping the capture replaced the whole set of camera settings, torch included.

**UI/UX**

- **The pairing panel drags.** The sheet carrying the pet's name, QR code and link now moves between three positions: full screen with the camera hidden, its normal resting height, or collapsed to a small handle with the camera filling the screen. It snaps to whichever is nearest when you let go. How much camera versus how much code has a different answer while you are aiming the phone at a cat bed than while reading the link out to someone.

**Internal**

- Fixed a crash in the release-AAB script when a build's commits all landed in one category.
