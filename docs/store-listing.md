## Full description (4000 chars max)

> **Tawny turns two phones into a cosy little pet monitor.**
>
> Leave one phone with your pet — an old handset on a charger is perfect. It
> becomes **the Watcher**: it points its camera at the room, sends the picture
> and the sound, and answers on its own. Keep the other phone with you as **the
> Handheld**: watch, listen in, hold a button to talk back, or play a gentle
> chime.
>
> **Set-up is a photo.** The Watcher shows a code; point the other phone's camera
> at it. No account, no sign-up, no cloud login.
>
> **It stays private.** On your home Wi-Fi, nothing leaves the house — one of
> your phones does the work a server would. Video and audio travel straight
> between your devices, encrypted end to end. Tawny has no ads, no analytics, and
> no tracking of any kind.
>
> **When you're out** (if this build was set up for it), Tawny quietly connects
> the two phones over the internet the same way — no ports to open, nothing to
> configure. The connecting service only introduces your devices; it never sees
> your video or your key.
>
> **What you get**
> • Live video and two-way audio
> • Push-to-talk, so the room isn't hearing your day
> • Bell / whistle / chirp chimes
> • A "dim" mode — glance at a blacked-out screen and still read the room's noise
> • Front/rear camera flip, mute, snapshot
> • Up to five Handhelds on one Watcher
>
> **Good to know:** Tawny streams only while the app is open on both phones. Keep
> the Watcher on a charger with the screen on (dim mode is fine). It is a pet
> monitor — please don't rely on it as a baby or safety monitor.
