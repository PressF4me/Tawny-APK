Tawny turns two phones into a cosy little pet monitor.

Leave one phone with your pet — an old handset on a charger is perfect. It
becomes the Monitor: it points its camera at the room, sends the picture and
the sound, and answers on its own. Keep the other phone with you as the Viewer:
watch, listen in, hold a button to talk back, or play a gentle chime.

SETTING UP IS A PHOTO
The Monitor shows a code. Point the other phone's camera at it. That's the
whole setup — no account, no sign-up, no cloud login, no address to type.

IT STAYS PRIVATE
On your home Wi-Fi nothing leaves the house: one of your own phones does the
job a server would. Video and audio travel straight between your devices,
encrypted end to end. Tawny has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any
kind. We never see your video, your sound, or your pairing key.

WHEN YOU'RE OUT
Tawny can introduce the two phones over the internet the same way — nothing to
configure and no ports to open. The service that introduces them only ever sees
a one-way hash of your key, never the key, and never your video. Some mobile
networks won't let two phones connect directly; when that happens Tawny falls
back to an encrypted relay, which forwards the stream without being able to read
it. Wi-Fi at either end is still the lightest path.

WHAT YOU GET
• Live video and two-way audio
• Hold-to-talk, so the room isn't listening to your whole day
• Call your pet over with a squeaky toy, a meow, a psp-psp-psp, a "good boy", or a bell
• Dim mode — glance at a blacked-out screen and still read the room's noise
• Front and rear camera, mute, zoom, snapshot
• Turn on the monitor phone's light from your own to see into a dark room
• Up to three Viewers watching one Monitor
• A short safety code to compare each time you connect from away
• Light and dark themes, or follow your phone’s setting
• A quick walkthrough for new users, plus in-app help and feedback

GOOD TO KNOW
Tawny streams only while the app is open on both phones. Keep the Monitor on a
charger with its screen on — dim mode is fine, and it stops the phone dozing
off. Tawny is a pet monitor: please don't rely on it as a baby monitor or as a
safety or security device.
